01 / THE CENTRAL IDEA

One view gives a line.
A new view gives a point.

A single configuration cannot distinguish contact points along the force line of action. A narrow particle cloud can look certain while missing the true contact.

Keep the ambiguity that the measurement cannot remove.

APCL restores hypotheses along the unresolved direction. Reorienting the grasped object then changes the force direction in the object frame, making the contact point identifiable across views.

(p + αf) × f = p × f

The same force and moment can correspond to an entire line of contact points.

How to read this replay

Coral shows CPF without recovery; mint shows full APCL recovery. On each robot, a colored diamond marks the estimated contact (the weighted particle mean), and the gold dot marks the true contact. Their 3D positions are to scale. Marker symbols are enlarged and drawn over the scene so they remain visible when occluded; the positions themselves are not displaced. The robots are translated apart for comparison, representing two independent replayed trials.

In the posterior plots, the gold dot is the true point and the white cross is the weighted estimate. Each plot uses its own object-frame projection onto the plane of its two force directions, centered on the true contact. Horizontal extent is ±250 mm; vertical extent is ±60 mm. The axis scales differ.

The motion is slowed and includes holds. The estimates and distributions change only at saved posterior snapshots; the film does not interpolate an invented particle trajectory. The box geometry is shown for context but is not given to the estimator. The gold arrow represents an applied point force, not a simulated physical pusher.

This episode’s trust statistic is 0.3503 (within c ≤ 0.87). It is a selected illustration of the recovery mechanism. The 1,200-trial results below provide aggregate evidence.

Transcript: Measure: both robots begin with the same contact scenario; one view leaves a line of possibilities. Preserve: APCL restores support along the unobservable direction. Move: each robot follows its own selected reorientation. Localize: the coral estimate remains displaced from the true point; the mint estimate lies close to it.

03 / THE METHOD

Measure what is observable.
Preserve what is not.

APCL combines residual validation, ambiguity-preserving contact inference, and active measurement. The object geometry and contact location are unknown.

A

Validate the residual

Estimate the payload contribution, then check the compensated momentum-observer residual on separate challenge poses.

Calibration + trust gate
B

Preserve hypotheses

Profile out the contact force and maintain contact particles. Recover from loss of support and retain candidates along the force line of action.

Force-profiled CPF + recovery
C

Choose the next view

Score feasible orientations with profile Fisher information. Change orientation while regulating the tool position, then update the estimate.

Active reorientation

The claim is about reliable contact hypotheses.

The particle filter provides a sequential representation of ambiguous contact locations. A batch re-estimate on the same windows can match its accuracy and give better-calibrated uncertainty. These results do not establish a universal accuracy or speed advantage over batch estimation.
